## Project overview

A whole-home technology package for the Clifford residence: a wired network
backbone sized for everything that rides on it, scene-based lighting control
in the main living spaces, music through the main floor and outdoor living
areas, a proper movie-night media room, and cameras covering the approaches
to the house. Everything is controlled from one app and engineered so the
family never has to think about it.

> **Read this first:** this intake arrived with the big three unspecified —
> room count, budget tier, and the core ask. We assumed a **six-room scope at
> a mid/upper budget tier** with a **whole-home foundation** as the core ask,
> and flagged every assumption in the last section. Tell us where we guessed
> wrong and we'll revise before anything is finalized.

## Assumptions & exclusions

Where the intake was silent, we assumed and flagged rather than holding the
project up. **The first three are the checklist-floor items the intake did
not specify — please confirm these first.**

1. **Room count (floor item, assumed):** six primary spaces — kitchen, family
   room, primary suite, office, media room, outdoor living.
2. **Budget tier (floor item, assumed):** mid/upper tier (~$50–60k). Good
   /better/best alternates available on request.
3. **Core ask (floor item, assumed):** whole-home foundation (network,
   lighting, audio, media room, surveillance) rather than a single-room
   project.
4. New construction or accessible attic/crawl assumed for wire runs; finished
   walls with no access would add retrofit labor.
5. Existing internet service (modem/ONT) assumed in place; ISP coordination
   excluded.
6. Lighting scope covers main spaces only, not every fixture in the house.
7. Motorized shades, distributed video, home theater (dedicated room), and
   integrated security/alarm are excluded but commonly added — ask us.
8. All product models are representative pending final match to the D-Tools
   catalog; equivalent-or-better substitutions may be made at equal or lower
   price.
9. Electrical work beyond low-voltage (new circuits, panel work) by owner's
   electrician.
