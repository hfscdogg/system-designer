## Project overview

A whole-home technology foundation for the Clifford residence, scoped in an
interview to **four spaces** — the kitchen/family room, the primary suite,
the home office, and the porch/patio — in a **finished home** (no open
walls), targeting the **$25–50k** tier with **all five systems kept and
value-engineered evenly** rather than any one dropped. The design is
wireless-first where it doesn't cost performance, so your finished walls and
ceilings stay finished.

## Assumptions & exclusions

The core ask, space list, budget tier, and site context were confirmed by
interview — they're decisions, not assumptions. What remains flagged:

1. Attic or crawl access is assumed available for the office network drop,
   patio speaker wiring, and exterior camera runs. If routes require wall
   opening and patching, that's a change order, not hidden labor.
2. The kitchen/family room is treated as one open zone; the media system is
   its music source. A separate kitchen speaker pair is an easy add.
3. Display sized at 75" pending a wall measurement; 65"/85" alternates
   reprice at promotion.
4. Existing internet service (modem/ONT) assumed in place; ISP coordination
   excluded.
5. Lighting covers the four in-scope spaces, not every fixture in the house.
6. Motorized shades, integrated security/alarm, and additional interior
   camera coverage are excluded but commonly added — ask us.
7. All product models are representative pending final match to the D-Tools
   catalog; equivalent-or-better substitutions may be made at equal or lower
   price.
8. Electrical work beyond low-voltage (new circuits, panel work) by owner's
   electrician.
